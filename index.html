About The Game

Catch The Button is a browser-based reflex game with one deceptively simple rule: click the glowing button before time runs out. Easy to learn — very hard to master.

How to Play

01

Start the Game

Click the green "START GAME" button to begin. The countdown starts immediately, every second counts!

02

Catch the Button

A glowing button appears in the field. Click it as fast as you can before the time runs out!

03

Earn Points

Each successful click earns you 1 point. The button instantly teleports to a new random position — chase it!

04

Survive the Difficulty

Every 10 points the button shrinks and moves faster. Stay sharp — can you reach INSANE in each game mode?

💡 Pro Tips

Stay calm — panicking causes missed clicks and wastes precious seconds. Watch the glowing edges of the button, not just its center. At higher levels, keep your cursor near the center to reduce travel distance. Every 10 points expect a difficulty jump — be mentally prepared. Practice short sessions to build muscle memory and reaction speed.
